Google's Cogentic posted six AI-generated theory papers, including claimed unconditional L[1/3] integer factoring. They ran on Gemini 4 with "no human interaction other than the initial prompt." Others claim sub-quasi-polynomial discrete logs in small-characteristic fields, deterministic subexponential factoring, and progress on the log-rank conjecture. The Lean proofs are promised but not posted, and nothing is peer reviewed. It's a proven bound, not faster practical factoring, and nothing verified threatens RSA-2048. The Lean and Mathlib communities certified Alpöge's Claude-assisted Lean proof of the Riemann Hypothesis. The Lean FRO and Mathlib Initiative endorsed the statement. The 17-million-line library compiles, two tools confirm the theorem is Mathlib's own RiemannHypothesis, and Lean's kernel plus the independent con-leche and con-ron checkers accept the proof. The GRH part covers only Dirichlet L-functions, and there's no human-readable proof yet. HN: 933 points. leanprover-community · unite.ai · HN
Alpöge and Buckmaster claim unforced 3D Navier–Stokes blowup, the version many experts consider the real Millennium problem. The Lean proves blowup only if a self-similar profile exists, and the certificate for that profile hasn't been released. Tao calls it "technically human-readable."
