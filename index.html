The Lean and Mathlib communities certified Alpöge's Claude-assisted Lean proof of the Riemann Hypothesis. The Lean FRO and Mathlib Initiative endorsed the statement. The 17-million-line library compiles, two tools confirm the theorem is Mathlib's own RiemannHypothesis, and Lean's kernel plus the independent con-leche and con-ron checkers accept the proof. The GRH part covers only Dirichlet L-functions, and there's no human-readable proof yet. HN: 933 points. leanprover-community · unite.ai · HN
