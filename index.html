Claude traced the 1997–98 NIST ECDSA curve seeds to a clock-seeded lrand48 in under 30 minutes. All ten prime-curve seeds (P-192 to P-521) came from Unix timestamps between May 1997 and April 1998, not hashed sentences. Anthropic: "not a cryptographic break." Because anyone can now reproduce the seeds, the result raises confidence that the curves have no backdoor. A $36,864 bounty went to ISRG. HN, 696 points. anthropic.com · HN
Claude Mythos 5 designed quantum circuits up to 17× smaller than the best published. Working in a closed loop with an automated evaluator, it beat prior designs on 8 primitives. Elliptic-curve point addition needs 17× fewer gates for 12% more qubits, and the DQI decoder about 12× fewer. Collaborators include Harvard's Yelin and Lukin. anthropic.com · unite.ai · HN
