"Encrypted" reasoning traces turned out to be readable across sibling models. Anthropic, OpenAI and Google all returned encrypted chain-of-thought blocks that are interchangeable across sessions, users and models in the same family — so you replay a frontier trace into a weaker sibling, jailbreak the sibling, and read the plaintext. The Haiku 4.5 prompt was one sentence. Worse than the distillation angle: the authors pulled 367 PII artifacts and 182 credentials from 315,320 reasoning blocks scraped out of public repos.
