OpenAI's chief scientist Jakub Pachocki proposed perplexity limits that labs would commit not to cross. In "Perplexity and Intelligence" he says log-perplexity on OpenAI's internal-code eval set fell from about 0.99 (GPT-3.5) to 0.91 (GPT-4) and below 0.80 now. He puts "transformative" capability around 0.68–0.70. He asks for shared reporting and "common limits… developers are committed to not crossing" until monitoring reaches a set milestone. HN commenters say the code-heavy eval set could explain the trend (542 points). openai.com · HN
