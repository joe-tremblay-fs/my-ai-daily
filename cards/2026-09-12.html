Twenty-five Fields Medallists signed a declaration that AI companies and mathematics are structurally misaligned — the day's biggest HN story at 779 points. Published by Terence Tao; signatories include Avila, Bhargava, Birkar, Deligne, Donaldson, Huh, Maynard, Scholze and Viazovska. The claim: "solving problems is only a tool and proxy for achieving the primary goal of conceptual understanding and insight." Results arrive "announced in a rush" faster than the field can verify, retractions get a fraction of the attention, and "the crucial human transmission chain between mathematicians would be lost."
