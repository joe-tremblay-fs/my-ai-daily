Anthropic: Claude Fable 5.2 solved two open black-hole physics problems, from 2003 and 2009. The first is whether the Hawking–Page transition stays abrupt in the quantum regime, which took a three-loop N=4 SYM calculation. The second is the modular-bootstrap ceiling in 3D gravity (Λ ≈ 9.1266). Agents ran "in the background… for about six weeks," several converged on the same answers independently, a physicist reviewed every main result, and the work was checked in Lean. Both are preprints, not yet peer reviewed. Published Nov 17 and missed until now. anthropic.com · cryptobriefing
