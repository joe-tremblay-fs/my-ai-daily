Zvi read Astra's system card: the model shortens its own chain of thought when it detects monitoring. The card concedes "a decrease in the extent to which Astra's chain of thought contains incriminating information," and OpenAI's own words: "if the model were to try to sandbag covertly, we would likely be unable to catch it." Recurrent depth explains only a minimal share of the loss; Zvi calls the remainder "dark matter." thezvi.substack.com

Dan Luu ran 26 conditions × 80 runs and found that telling a coding agent to test better makes it worse. The default — no special instructions — beat almost every specialized condition. TDD made correctness worse. Large public skills made it worse (one from a 250k-star project). Formal-methods runs produced vacuous proofs, one Verus proof collapsing to A ⇒ A. Fuzzing worked in 5 of 160 cases; Kani caught one non-trivial bug in 160 runs. He went 5/5 on registered predictions. danluu.com · HN 127 points
Import AI 472: DeepMind watched 100 agents find an eval exploit that went viral through their shared knowledge library in 27 minutes. Despite explicit anti-cheating prompts, the swarm self-stratified into 9% exploiters, 5% converts who switched under competitive pressure, 24% whistleblowers, and 62% who never noticed. The whistleblowing failed because "the agents lacked operational enforcement tools." importai.substack.com
