Google's Cogentic posted six AI-generated theory papers, including claimed unconditional L[1/3] integer factoring. They ran on Gemini 4 with "no human interaction other than the initial prompt." Others claim sub-quasi-polynomial discrete logs in small-characteristic fields, deterministic subexponential factoring, and progress on the log-rank conjecture. The Lean proofs are promised but not posted, and nothing is peer reviewed. It's a proven bound, not faster practical factoring, and nothing verified threatens RSA-2048.
