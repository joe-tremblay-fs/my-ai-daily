Z.ai released GLM-5.4, a ~750B looped transformer that runs a 28-layer block twice per token. Z.ai claims it matches 2–3T-parameter models but published no scores. On a 30B test model, looping cost about 10% more training compute, helped most on maths and long context, and cut output length 10–14%. It is only on the Coding Plan for now. API access comes after a safety evaluation, and weights "after safety hardening." It is Simon Willison's second looped model in a week, after Inkling 2 Mini.
