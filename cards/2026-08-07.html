The same 24 hours: AI-designed viruses stopped being hypothetical. Stanford and the Arc Institute published in Science that Evo 1 and Evo 2 — trained on 2.7M genomes — wrote whole phage genomes from scratch; of ~300 synthesized, 16 were viable, some replicating faster than the ΦX174 parent, and a cocktail cleared drug-resistant E. coli. Trained only on non-human pathogens as a safety control. This is the capability Anthropic's classifier is calibrated against, demonstrated the day the calibration moved.
