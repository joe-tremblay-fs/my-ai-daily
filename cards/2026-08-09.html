Anthropic is making auto mode the default in Claude Code — and the argument is that humans are the weak link. From Aug 14, Pro, Max and Team sessions start with permission prompts off; Claude proceeds unless an action is irreversible, destructive, or aimed outside your environment. The evidence: across 1,053 paid testers, human review caught 13.6% of dangerous commands and auto mode caught 89% — because users approve 97% of permission prompts, so the prompt stopped being a decision. Classifier overhead is dropped for those tiers, so the cheaper option is now the default. Ships with hard denies on exfiltration, prompt-injection screening, and git-status awareness. Enterprise/API stay opt-in. claude.com/blog · TechCrunch

Zvi and Nathan Lambert wrote the two definitive takes on the OpenAI incident, and they converge.
